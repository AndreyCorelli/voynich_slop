# Grammar restoration: proposed modifications

This living tracker records proposals, evidence and adoption status. Frozen iteration reports and artifacts remain unchanged. Results on reused development partitions do not constitute fresh confirmation.

**Objective:** solid grammatical restoration is an achievement independently of exact lexical recovery. Assess grammatical accuracy, uncertainty, coverage and downstream structure directly; word-identity success is not a prerequisite.

**Finding to preserve — iteration 65: competition-aware abstention improves exact-unit F1.**

[Joint subject selection with NONE](grammar_joint_subject_none_iteration65.md) is the strongest recent positive scoring/decoding result: **56.86%→58.89% full-source F1; 64.85%→66.87% selected**, with gains in all five folds. Fine global-threshold controls do not reproduce it; drop-only competition retains most of the benefit. Preserve the frozen independent baseline, the primary carrier-calibrated competition policy, both controls, and their verification manifests as reference points for subsequent experiments.

This is an **experimental precision/F1 option**, not a default or an increase in recovered structures: 427 fewer false units, but 72 fewer correct units net; coordination recovery falls from 48 to 39/122. The reserve remains unscored.

**Promising follow-up — iteration 68:** [relative conjunct evidence confined to contested carriers](grammar_conj_competition_evidence_iteration68.md) raises F1 **58.89%→59.41% source; 66.87%→67.53% selected**, adds10 correct units and removes37 false ones relative to65, and partly restores coordination **39→43/122**. The POS-only control is nearly tied overall and better on selected sentences, but does not improve coordination. Preserve both68 variants as experimental candidates; context is not a demonstrated general winner. [Oracle66](grammar_coordination_oracle_iteration66.md) recovers all nine losses; [global learned67](grammar_conj_subject_evidence_iteration67.md) fails coverage/selected-F1 guards.
