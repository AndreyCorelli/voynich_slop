# Progress — 2026-10-26


## Daily summary

We have promising **selective grammatical relations**, but no reliable exact Latin nuclei, named-POS restoration, or Voynich translation. The practical output to investigate is a small set of soft compatibility preferences between opaque words, with coverage, supervision and uncertainty kept explicit.

- **More Latin material:** the collection grew to roughly one million annotated token rows across six treebanks; admission, deduplication and licensing qualifications are recorded, and reserved material is not interchangeable with development data. [Collection report](../latin_source_expansion_20261026.md).
- **Trained relational selection:** a source-trained ranker selects ten endpoint-disjoint pairs with mean reference POS-profile compatibility of **79% Aquinas, 68% Caesar, 79% Cicero and 89% Jerome**; the target author's labels are excluded from fitting and selection. These are compatibility scores, not named-POS accuracy or calibrated per-pair confidence, and source-label controls do not establish robust learned improvement in every author. [Selection and controls](../grammar_pos_edge_robustness_iteration226.md).
- **Contextual morphology:** Dante retains **92.98% conditional Case equality** above word×POS-profile controls, and localized bundles survive a central-occurrence deletion in several datasets. These findings use reference annotations to establish what the relations mean; they do not identify named cases or all applicable positions unaided. [Case controls](../grammar_dante_pos_profile_iteration200.md), [bundle robustness](../grammar_pivot_deletion_iteration205.md).
- **Full-text success, limited reproducibility:** a six-word Jerome group is entirely VERB in the reference and exceeds all 199 matched full-pipeline order controls (finite tail **0.005**). However, the old strict extractor produces no recurring group across chronological halves or 95 random sentence bipartitions, so full-text robustness does not establish passage-level recovery. [Full controls](../grammar_full_core_null_iteration242.md), [half-text failures](../grammar_random_halves_iteration246.md).
- **A concrete representation repair:** equal-weight full-basis embeddings erased distances in **70/145 eligible half-graphs**; fixed time-one diffusion removes that collapse and improves selected Jerome-pair closeness from **55.67% to 95.31%** when endpoints are available. Those figures are distance ranks, not grammatical accuracy. [Diagnosis](../grammar_pair_proximity_iteration248.md), [repair](../grammar_diffusion_proximity_iteration249.md).
- **Selective recurrence, still rare:** diffusion clustering finds two anonymous Aquinas groups where all 19 matched shuffled corpora produce no shared core, despite repeating all 19 splits per control (finite tail **0.05**). On unused splits the fixed AUX pair recurs only **1/19** times and the fixed ADP-dominant triple **0/19**; a subsequent stable mutual-neighbor rule adds a reference-pure Jerome ADV pair in **2/19** splits but still needs its own matched controls. [Matched pilot](../grammar_diffusion_controls_iteration251.md), [fixed-group validation](../grammar_fixed_groups_iteration252.md), [local rule](../grammar_mutual_neighbors_iteration253.md).

No general 70%-accurate POS tagger has emerged: the direct 17-category test gives only **12.85–33.08% type-macro expected correctness** on its covered vocabulary. Stronger historical binary function-word classifications require a supplied candidate set, and the phrase-membership successes require supplied information; neither is blind grammatical recovery from an unknown opaque Latin text. [Direct POS test](../grammar_pos_prediction_iteration231.md), [candidate-discovery failure](../grammar_candidate_detection_iteration155.md).

## Best shot today

The best current route is to give another decoder a small ranked set of **anonymous grammatical compatibility relations**, using the trained pair ranker as a candidate component rather than demanding a complete dictionary or named POS first. Its ten-pair shortlists achieve roughly **68–89% mean reference POS-profile compatibility across four authors**, with restricted coverage and uneven learned gains beyond simple controls. Fixed diffusion and stable local neighbors are promising refinements, but rare recurrence, false full-text pairs and missing matched validation for the newest rule prevent treating their outputs as hard constraints.

## Next step

Run matched word-order controls for the stable mutual-neighbor rule on both Aquinas and Jerome, retaining every split, class and empty output; then test the new Jerome pair on unused splits if the controls are favorable. Preserve the trained ranking and conditional morphological findings as separate possible inputs rather than replacing them with whichever experiment is newest. All results remain development evidence, and the original shared exact-word nucleus objective is unmet.
