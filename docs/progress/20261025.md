## Frozen sentence-free class replication on Gellius and Livy

[Iteration 138](../grammar_overlap_raw_latin_iteration138.md) applies the existing anchor-free, sentence-free overlap classifier unchanged to already inspected Latin word streams. Caesar supplies 52,691 tokens versus 7,713–16,983 elsewhere, so token-matched checks are the next proposed diagnostic. Candidate membership remains supplied, and raw Gellius `c` may not share the treebank conjunction reference; all present types remain in the predeclared results.

Preserve the earlier Caesar signal and weaker Gellius order-sensitive ranking, alongside this failed broad transfer. No exact word identities or shared lexical nuclei are recovered, and no model is adopted as a default. Independent audit and fifteen focused tests pass; see the [high-level goal](../high_level_goal.md) and [proposal tracker](../grammar_algorithm_proposals.md).

## Selective confidence does not provide a transferable class seed

## Complementary role-ranking signal, with a calibration failure

[Iteration 141](../grammar_context_roles_iteration141.md) uses one anchor-free second-order neighbor-role cosine statistic. It improves **Gellius AUC to 0.9107** and **Livy to 0.7083**, above all sixteen frequency-preserving nulls, but loses Caesar/Jerome separation. The calibrated decision boundary lies outside the feasible feature range, forcing every prediction to CCONJ; reject it as a replacement while preserving the new ranking evidence separately from the earlier Caesar overlap finding. A small source-only joint model with domain checks is proposed; no reliable class seed or exact lexical nuclei are established.

## Milestone: one anchor-free joint model reaches 71–80% across four texts

**Preserve [iteration 142](../grammar_joint_scalars_iteration142.md):** the predeclared uncalibrated joint model combines two scalar word-order statistics and gets **Caesar 12/15 (80%), Jerome 12/16 (75%), Gellius 12/15 (80%), and Livy 10/14 (71.43%)**.
